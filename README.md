# Franquero Institute for Quantum-Thermodynamic Neuroscience (FIQTN)

Welcome to the official open repository of the **Franquero Institute for Quantum-Thermodynamic Neuroscience (FIQTN)**. This repository houses the computational models, differential equations, documentation, and preprint resources associated with our core research frameworks.

---

## Research Pillars

### 1. FMQC (Franquero Model of Quantum-Thermodynamic Consciousness)
* **Overview:** Explores how the human brain functions as an active biological refrigerator. By utilizing astrocytic cooling loops, fluid dynamics (CSF), and entropy dissipation through the **Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB)**, the brain protects delicate quantum states from thermal decoherence at 37°C.
* **Core Focus:** Mathematical modeling of thermodynamic thresholds ($37.8^\circ\text{C}$), macroscopic quantum stability, and synchronization collapse ($R$).

### 2. FDF (Franquero Dynamics Framework)
* **Overview:** A quantitative computational model for multi-scale dynamics, originally established for temporal optimization of immunotherapy (glycocalyx modification, costimulation, and checkpoint inhibition) and expanding into complex biological systems dynamics.
* **Core Focus:** Non-linear dose-response functions, multi-scale temporal coordination, and predictive computational architecture.
